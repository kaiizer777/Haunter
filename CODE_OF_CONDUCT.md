# Code of Conduct

Haunter is built by people who review each other's production code. Hold a high bar for the work and a generous one for the humans doing it.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and professional in issues, PRs, reviews, and chat.
- Critique code, not people. Comment on the diff ("this query N+1s on large repos"), never the author.
- Assume good faith; ask a clarifying question before accusing anyone of bad intent.
- Own mistakes plainly: if you broke `main` or shipped a bad review, say so, revert or fix forward, and note the lesson.
- Give actionable reviews: state the problem, why it matters, and what "good" looks like. Distinguish must-fix from nitpick.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs, personal attacks, or threats — in any channel.
- Dismissing or demeaning contributions based on experience level, background, or identity.
- Publishing private information (real names, emails, credentials) without consent.
- Faking test results, review approvals, or authorship — including AI-generated content presented as manually verified.

## Enforcement

- Reviews and discussion stay focused on the work; maintainers may hide or remove comments that attack people rather than code.
- Repeated or severe violations may lead to blocked contributions or removal from the project.
- Report concerns privately to the maintainers with links or screenshots; reports are kept confidential.
